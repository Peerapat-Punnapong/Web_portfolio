P/02 Grabspark 2026

Grab Turtle (TAO)

Grabspark 2026 · Team Four Turtle

  • Project Manager
  • UX/UI Designer
  • Prototype via vibe coding

An AI profit assistant for small restaurants that sell both in-store and on GrabFood. Scan a receipt or log income and expenses, and it turns the break-even point into one simple daily goal: “how many boxes do I need to sell today?” A wise master turtle explains it all in plain Thai. Designed from a survey of 50 real restaurants.

P/04 Personal project

Internship Corporation for ICT

Personal project · 2026 · For Mahidol ICT students

  • Full-stack Developer

A shared directory of internship companies for Mahidol ICT students. Search by company, position or tag, sort by closing date or review score, save favourites and read reviews from seniors. Anyone can share a company in under a minute; an admin dashboard approves submissions, manages companies, moderates reviews and handles error reports. Hosted by Memo, a friendly robot mascot.
